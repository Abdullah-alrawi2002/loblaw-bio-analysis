Method and interpretation

The primary analysis averages each subject’s sample percentages across days 0, 7, and 14. Two-sided Mann–Whitney U tests compare independent subjects using asymptotic p-values with tie and continuity corrections. Holm correction controls family-wise error across the five populations, including when the tests are dependent.

Day-specific results are exploratory, with a separate five-test correction per visit. There is no correction across all selectable time views. These unadjusted associations do not establish causality or predictive performance. Subject averages include post-treatment information; baseline comparisons avoid that information. Percentages describe composition among these five populations and cannot establish absolute cell changes.
